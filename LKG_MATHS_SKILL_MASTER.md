## Complete 55 Micro-Skill Syllabus (A.1 to J.2)

### Category A: Shapes (6 Worksheets)
- **A.1**: Name the shape (Circle, Square, Triangle, Rectangle)
- **A.2**: Circles (Round shape identification, circular real-world objects)
- **A.3**: Squares (4 equal sides and 4 corners)
- **A.4**: Triangles (3 sides and 3 corners)
- **A.5**: Rectangles (2 long sides, 2 short sides)
- **A.6**: Circles, squares and triangles (Comparative geometric sorting)

### Category B: Count to 3 (7 Worksheets)
- **B.1**: Learn to count - up to 3 (Sequential counting: 1, 2, 3)
- **B.2**: Count objects - up to 3 (Counting apples, ducks, balls)
- **B.3**: Count dots - up to 3 (Dice dot patterns and domino arrays)
- **B.4**: Count shapes - up to 3 (Geometric shape counting)
- **B.5**: Count on ten frames - up to 3 (Ten-frame counters 1 to 3)
- **B.6**: Show numbers on ten frames - up to 3 (Select the correct ten-frame display)
- **B.7**: Represent numbers - up to 3 (Matching numerals to finger counts and tally marks)

### Category C: Count to 5 (7 Worksheets)
- **C.1**: Learn to count - up to 5 (Sequential counting: 1 to 5)
- **C.2**: Count objects - up to 5 (Counting animals, stars, cookies)
- **C.3**: Count dots - up to 5 (Dot patterns up to 5)
- **C.4**: Count shapes - up to 5 (Shape collections up to 5)
- **C.5**: Count on ten frames - up to 5 (Ten-frame counter filling top row)
- **C.6**: Show numbers on ten frames - up to 5 (Ten-frame matching)
- **C.7**: Represent numbers - up to 5 (Number matching to five-frame arrays)

### Category D: Count to 10 (7 Worksheets)
- **D.1**: Learn to count - up to 10 (Counting sequence 6, 7, 8, 9, 10)
- **D.2**: Count objects - up to 10 (Counting collections up to 10)
- **D.3**: Count dots - up to 10 (Two-row dot patterns)
- **D.4**: Count shapes - up to 10 (10-shape arrays)
- **D.5**: Count on ten frames - up to 10 (Full and partially full ten-frames)
- **D.6**: Show numbers on ten frames - up to 10 (Match numeral to two-row ten-frame)
- **D.7**: Represent numbers - up to 10 (Numerals, hands, objects representation)

### Category E: Comparing (5 Worksheets)
- **E.1**: Are there enough? (One-to-one correspondence: bunnies to carrots, cups to saucers)
- **E.2**: More (Identifying groups with more items)
- **E.3**: Fewer (Identifying groups with fewer items)
- **E.4**: Fewer and more - compare by counting (Counting both groups and comparing)
- **E.5**: Compare in a mixed group (Comparing counts in mixed object arrangements)

### Category F: Positions (7 Worksheets)
- **F.1**: Inside and outside (Identifying objects inside vs outside a box/basket)
- **F.2**: Above and below (Identifying objects flying above vs swimming below)
- **F.3**: Beside and next to (Identifying objects standing beside or adjacent)
- **F.4**: Left and right (Spatial orientation: left hand / right hand side)
- **F.5**: Left, middle and right (3-object sequence: finding the middle item)
- **F.6**: Top and bottom (Shelf layers: top shelf vs bottom shelf)
- **F.7**: Top, middle and bottom (3-tier stack: top, middle, bottom)

### Category G: Classify (6 Worksheets)
- **G.1**: Same (Identifying two objects that are exactly identical)
- **G.2**: Different (Spotting the odd one out in a set)
- **G.3**: Same and different (Comparative matching of attributes)
- **G.4**: Classify shapes by colour (Grouping all blue items or all red items)
- **G.5**: Classify and sort by colour (Sorting multi-colored objects into matching bins)
- **G.6**: Classify and sort by shape (Sorting circles into circle bin, squares into square bin)

### Category H: Patterns (4 Worksheets)
- **H.1**: Colour patterns (AB and AAB repeating color sequences: Red-Blue-Red-Blue)
- **H.2**: Size patterns (Big-Small-Big-Small alternating sequences)
- **H.3**: Shape patterns (Circle-Square-Circle-Square alternating sequences)
- **H.4**: What comes next? (Predicting the next element in repeating patterns)

### Category I: Size (4 Worksheets)
- **I.1**: Long and short (Comparing pencil lengths, snake lengths, trains)
- **I.2**: Tall and short (Comparing giraffe vs turtle, tower vs hut)
- **I.3**: Wide and narrow (Comparing doors, roads, ribbons)
- **I.4**: Light and heavy (Comparing feather vs rock, elephant vs butterfly)

### Category J: Money (2 Worksheets)
- **J.1**: Coin values (Recognizing ₹1, ₹2, ₹5, ₹10 Indian currency coins)
- **J.2**: Count 1-rupee coins (Counting 1-rupee coins to make ₹2, ₹3, ₹4, ₹5)

## Pedagogical Principles & Quality Assurance
1. **Zero Spoilers**: Questions never give away the answer in the title, prompt, or distractors.
2. **Audio-Friendly Prompts**: Each question is designed to sound natural when spoken through speech synthesis in both Hindi and English.
3. **Universal Visuals**: Universal emojis ensure perfect cross-platform rendering on Android, iOS, Windows, and web browsers.
4. **Instant Positive Feedback**: Every question has a clear, encouraging explanation with real-world context.
