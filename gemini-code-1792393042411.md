## 5. Core Game Loop: Fastest Finger First (FFF) + Hot Seat + Spectator Mode

### Step A: 10-Player Pool & Smart Bot Matchmaking
- When a player taps **"Play Now"**, they enter a 10-player lobby for their Division.
- **5-Second Matchmaking Timer:** The system searches for live players for 5 seconds.
- **Smart Auto-Fill Bots:** Remaining empty slots (up to 10 total) are automatically filled with realistic division-matched System Bots (e.g., *"Aarav • Delhi"*, *"Zoya • Mumbai"*).
- **Single-Player Safeguard:** If only 1 real human is in the pool with 9 bots, the bots' FFF response time is dynamically set slightly slower than the human's (or calibrated by Admin settings) so the real player wins the FFF round and reaches the Hot Seat.

### Step B: Fastest Finger First (FFF) Round
- All 10 players get 1 chronological/ordering question (e.g., Arrange 4 events/numbers in correct order within 15 seconds).
- The fastest accurate player moves to the **Main Hot Seat**.

### Step C: Spectator Mode & Live Audience Poll (For the Remaining 9 Players)
- Players who do not make the Hot Seat are NOT kicked out. They transition into the **Live Studio Audience** for that match (or can exit anytime).
- **Interactive Spectator Features:**
  - Watch the Hot-Seat player's live progress on the 15-question ladder.
  - Send live floating emoji reactions (Clap, Fire, Thinking, Thumbs Up).
  - **Live Audience Poll Participation:** When the Hot-Seat player triggers the *"Audience Poll"* lifeline, a 10-second voting prompt pops up on spectators' screens. Spectators who vote for the correct option earn **+10 Bonus XP**!

### Step D: The 15-Question Hot Seat Ladder
- **15 Levels of Progression:** Starting from Level 1 (1,000 Points) up to Level 15 (1 Crore Virtual Points).
- **Safe Padavs (Milestones):** Question 5 (10,000 Points) and Question 10 (3,20,000 Points). Falling after a Padav guarantees the milestone points.
- **4 Lifelines:**
  1. **50:50:** Removes two wrong options.
  2. **Audience Poll:** Uses live spectator votes + calibrated bot votes to show a percentage bar graph.
  3. **Ask the AI Guru:** Displays a crisp 1-line smart hint/explanation.
  4. **Time Freeze:** Stops the countdown clock for the current question.
- **Question timers:** Q1–Q5 (30 sec), Q6–Q10 (45 sec), Q11–Q15 (60 sec).
